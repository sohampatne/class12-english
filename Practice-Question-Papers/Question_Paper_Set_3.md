Only a mind that can resist the tyrannical frenzy of the immediate can formulate principled ethical judgments, discern nuance amidst polarized hysteria, and sustain the patient courage required for systemic reform. Silence is the fertile soil from which genuine courage, profound artistic expression, and lasting wisdom spring forth.
